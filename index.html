Calibration

Play steady noise or a 1 kHz tone near a trusted meter, select the same weighting here, enter its reading and tap Calibrate. Until then values are dBFS (relative to digital full scale), not dB SPL.

Limits: phone mics clip at high sound levels and roll off at low frequency, so check both before trusting readings. Calibration holds only near the level and spectrum used. Peak is the highest C-weighted sample. Bands are summed from FFT bins, so the lowest bands contain only a few bins.
